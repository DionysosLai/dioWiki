## 2.2 Improving Efficiency

> ## 2.2 提高效率

Given an unbiased Monte Carlo estimator, we are in the fortunate position of having a reliable relationship between the number of samples taken and variance (and thus, error). If we have an unacceptably noisy rendered image, increasing the number of samples will reduce error in a predictable way, and—given enough computation—an image of sufficient quality can be generated.

> 一旦拥有一个无偏的蒙特卡洛估计量，我们就获得了一件很幸运的事：样本数量与方差（也因此与误差）之间存在可靠关系。如果渲染图像的噪声高到无法接受，那么只要增加样本数量，误差就会以可预期的方式下降；并且——只要计算资源足够——最终总能生成质量足够好的图像。

However, computation takes time, and often there is not enough of it. The deadline for a movie may be at hand, or the sixtieth-of-a-second time slice in a real-time renderer may be coming to an end. Given the consequentially limited number of samples, the only option for variance reduction is to find ways to make more of the samples that can be taken. Fortunately, a variety of techniques have been developed to improve the basic Monte Carlo estimator by making the most of the samples that are taken; here we will discuss the most important ones that are used in pbrt.

> 然而计算需要时间，而时间往往并不充裕。电影出片的截止日期可能近在眼前，或者实时渲染器里六十分之一秒的时间片也快要耗尽。在样本数因此受到限制的情况下，降低方差的唯一办法，就是想办法让已经能取得的样本发挥更大作用。幸运的是，人们已经发展出多种技术，通过尽可能榨取已有样本的价值来改进基本的蒙特卡洛估计量；这里我们将讨论其中在 pbrt 中最重要的几种。

### 2.2.1 Stratified Sampling

> ### 2.2.1 分层采样

A classic and effective family of techniques for variance reduction is based on the careful placement of samples in order to better capture the features of the integrand (or, more accurately, to be less likely to miss important features). These techniques are used extensively in pbrt. Stratified sampling decomposes the integration domain into regions and places samples in each one; here we will analyze that approach in terms of its variance reduction properties. Later, in Section [8.2.1](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Sampling_and_Reconstruction/Sampling_and_Integration.html#sec:fourier-analysis-variance), we will return with machinery based on Fourier analysis that provides further insights about it.

> 一类经典而有效的降方差技术，建立在“更仔细地放置样本”之上，从而更好地捕捉被积函数的特征（更准确地说，是更不容易漏掉重要特征）。这些技术在 pbrt 中被广泛使用。分层采样会把积分域分解为若干区域，并在每个区域中放置样本；这里我们将从它降低方差的性质来分析这种方法。稍后在第 [8.2.1](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Sampling_and_Reconstruction/Sampling_and_Integration.html#sec:fourier-analysis-variance) 节中，我们还会借助傅里叶分析工具再次回到这个话题，获得更进一步的理解。

Stratified sampling subdivides the integration domain \(\Lambda\) into \(n\) nonoverlapping regions \(\Lambda_1, \Lambda_2, \ldots, \Lambda_n\). Each region is called a *stratum*, and they must completely cover the original domain:

> 分层采样把积分域 \(\Lambda\) 划分成 \(n\) 个互不重叠的区域 \(\Lambda_1, \Lambda_2, \ldots, \Lambda_n\)。每个区域称为一个*层*（stratum），并且它们必须完整覆盖原始定义域：

$$
\bigcup_{i=1}^{n} \Lambda_i = \Lambda
$$

To draw samples from \(\Lambda\), we will draw \(n_i\) samples from each \(\Lambda_i\), according to densities \(p_i\) inside each stratum. A simple example is supersampling a pixel. With stratified sampling, the area around a pixel is divided into a \(k \times k\) grid, and a sample is drawn uniformly within each grid cell. This is better than taking \(k^2\) random samples, since the sample locations are less likely to clump together. Here we will show why this technique reduces variance.

> 为了从 \(\Lambda\) 中取样，我们会在每个 \(\Lambda_i\) 中按照该层内部的密度 \(p_i\) 抽取 \(n_i\) 个样本。一个简单例子是像素超采样：使用分层采样时，像素周围区域会被划分为一个 \(k \times k\) 网格，并在每个网格单元内部均匀地取一个样本。这比直接取 \(k^2\) 个纯随机样本更好，因为样本位置更不容易扎堆。下面我们就来说明为什么这种技术能够降低方差。

Within a single stratum \(\Lambda_i\), the Monte Carlo estimate is

> 在单个层 \(\Lambda_i\) 内，蒙特卡洛估计量为：

$$
F_i = \frac{1}{n_i} \sum_{j=1}^{n_i} \frac{f(X_{i,j})}{p_i(X_{i,j})}
$$

where \(X_{i,j}\) is the \(j\)th sample drawn from density \(p_i\). The overall estimate is \(F = \sum_i v_i F_i\), where \(v_i\) is the fractional volume of stratum \(i\) \((v_i \in (0,1])\).

> 其中 \(X_{i,j}\) 表示从密度 \(p_i\) 中抽出的第 \(j\) 个样本。整体估计量是 \(F = \sum_i v_i F_i\)，其中 \(v_i\) 表示第 \(i\) 个层所占的体积分数（\(v_i \in (0,1]\)）。

The true value of the integrand in stratum \(i\) is

> 第 \(i\) 个层中被积函数的真实均值为：

$$
\mu_i = \frac{1}{v_i} \int_{\Lambda_i} f(x)\,dx
$$

and the variance in this stratum is

> 而该层中的方差为：

$$
\sigma_i^2 = \frac{1}{v_i} \int_{\Lambda_i} (f(x) - \mu_i)^2\,dx
$$

Thus, with \(n_i\) samples in the stratum, the variance of the per-stratum estimator is \(\sigma_i^2 / n_i\). This shows that the variance of the overall estimator is

> 因此，如果该层中有 \(n_i\) 个样本，那么该层估计量的方差就是 \(\sigma_i^2 / n_i\)。于是整体估计量的方差为：

$$
V[F] = \sum_i v_i^2 V[F_i] = \sum_i \frac{v_i^2 \sigma_i^2}{n_i}
$$

If we make the reasonable assumption that the number of samples is proportional to the volume, then we have \(n_i = v_i n\), and the variance of the overall estimator is

> 如果做一个合理的假设：每层分配到的样本数与它的体积分数成正比，那么就有 \(n_i = v_i n\)，此时整体估计量的方差为：

$$
V[F_n] = \frac{1}{n} \sum_i v_i \sigma_i^2
$$

To compare this result to the variance without stratification, we note that choosing an unstratified sample is equivalent to choosing a random stratum \(I\) according to the discrete probability distribution defined by the volumes \(v_i\) and then choosing a random sample \(X\) in \(\Lambda_I\). In this sense, \(X\) is chosen *conditionally* on \(I\), so it can be shown using conditional probability that

> 为了把这个结果与“不分层时”的方差作比较，我们注意到：选取一个非分层样本，等价于先按照由各层体积分数 \(v_i\) 定义的离散概率分布随机选中某个层 \(I\)，然后再在 \(\Lambda_I\) 中随机选取一个样本 \(X\)。从这个角度看，\(X\) 是在给定 \(I\) 的条件下被选出的，因此可以用条件概率证明：

([2.12](#eq:strat-variance))

$$
V[F] = \frac{1}{n}\left[\sum_i v_i \sigma_i^2 + \sum_i v_i (\mu_i - Q)^2\right]
$$

where \(Q\) is the mean of \(f\) over the whole domain.

> 其中 \(Q\) 表示函数 \(f\) 在整个定义域上的平均值。

There are two things to notice about Equation ([2.12](#eq:strat-variance)). First, we know that the right-hand sum must be nonnegative, since variance is always nonnegative. Second, it demonstrates that stratified sampling can never increase variance. Stratification always reduces variance unless the right-hand sum is exactly 0. It can only be 0 when the function has the same mean over each stratum. For stratified sampling to work best, we would like to maximize the right-hand sum, so it is best to make the strata have means that are as unequal as possible. This explains why *compact* strata are desirable if one does not know anything about the function. If the strata are wide, they will contain more variation and will have closer to the true mean.

> 关于式（[2.12](#eq:strat-variance)），有两点值得注意。第一，右侧第二项一定是非负的，因为方差本身永远非负。第二，这个式子说明：分层采样永远不会增加方差。除非右侧第二项恰好为 0，否则分层总会降低方差。而它只有在函数在每个层上的均值都相同时才会变成 0。为了让分层采样尽可能发挥作用，我们希望让右侧第二项尽量大，因此最好让不同层的均值尽可能不一样。这也解释了为什么当我们对函数一无所知时，更希望层是*紧凑*的：如果层划得太宽，内部变化会更大，各层均值也会更接近全局真实均值。

Figure [2.1](#fig:mc-strat-images) shows the effect of using stratified sampling versus an independent random distribution for sampling when rendering an image that includes glossy reflection. There is a reasonable reduction in variance at essentially no cost in running time.

> 图 [2.1](#fig:mc-strat-images) 展示了在渲染带有光泽反射的图像时，使用分层采样与使用独立随机采样之间的差别。它在几乎不增加运行时间的前提下，实现了相当可观的方差下降。

Figure 2.1: Variance is higher and the image noisier (a) when independent random sampling is used than (b) when a stratified distribution of sample directions is used instead. *(Bunny model courtesy of the Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory.)*

> 图 2.1：使用独立随机采样时（a）方差更高、图像噪声更重；而使用分层的方向采样分布时（b）效果更稳定。*（Bunny 模型由 Stanford Computer Graphics Laboratory 提供。）*

The main downside of stratified sampling is that it suffers from the same “curse of dimensionality” as standard numerical quadrature. Full stratification in \(D\) dimensions with \(S\) strata per dimension requires \(S^D\) samples, which quickly becomes prohibitive. Fortunately, it is often possible to stratify some of the dimensions independently and then randomly associate samples from different dimensions; this approach will be used in Section [8.5](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Sampling_and_Reconstruction/Stratified_Sampler.html#sec:stratified-sampling). Choosing which dimensions are stratified should be done in a way that stratifies dimensions that tend to be most highly correlated in their effect on the value of the integrand ([Owen 1998](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/Further_Reading.html#cite:owen98latin)).

> 分层采样的主要缺点在于，它和标准数值求积一样，也会遭遇“维度灾难”。如果在 \(D\) 维空间中每一维都分成 \(S\) 个层，那么完全分层就需要 \(S^D\) 个样本，很快就会变得不可承受。幸运的是，很多时候我们可以只对部分维度独立地分层，然后再把不同维度的样本随机配对；第 [8.5](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Sampling_and_Reconstruction/Stratified_Sampler.html#sec:stratified-sampling) 节会用到这种方法。至于应该对哪些维度做分层，理想的做法是优先分层那些对被积函数取值影响高度相关的维度（[Owen 1998](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/Further_Reading.html#cite:owen98latin)）。

### 2.2.2 Importance Sampling

> ### 2.2.2 重要性采样

Importance sampling is a powerful variance reduction technique that exploits the fact that the Monte Carlo estimator

> 重要性采样是一种非常强大的降方差技术，它利用了下面这个事实：对于蒙特卡洛估计量

$$
F_n = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{f(X_i)}{p(X_i)}
$$

converges more quickly if the samples are taken from a distribution that is similar to the function in the integrand. In this case, samples are more likely to be taken when the magnitude of the integrand is relatively large. Importance sampling is one of the most frequently used variance reduction techniques in rendering, since it is easy to apply and is very effective when good sampling distributions are used.

> 如果样本来自一个与被积函数形状相近的分布，那么它的收敛会更快。这样一来，被积函数数值较大的区域会更容易被采到。重要性采样是渲染中最常见的降方差技术之一，因为它实现简单，而且一旦采样分布选得好，效果往往非常显著。

To see why such sampling distributions reduce error, first consider the effect of using a distribution \(p(x) \propto f(x)\), or \(p(x) = c f(x)\). It is trivial to show that normalization of the PDF requires that

> 为了说明为什么这样的采样分布能降低误差，先考虑使用 \(p(x) \propto f(x)\) 的分布，也就是 \(p(x) = c f(x)\) 的情形。很容易证明，为了让 PDF 归一化，必须满足：

$$
c = \frac{1}{\int f(x)\,dx}
$$

Finding such a PDF requires that we know the value of the integral, which is what we were trying to estimate in the first place. Nonetheless, if we *could* sample from this distribution, each term of the sum in the estimator would have the value

> 要构造这样一个 PDF，就需要提前知道积分值本身，而这恰恰正是我们原本想估计的量。不过，如果我们*真的能*从这个分布里采样，那么估计量求和中的每一项都会变成：

$$
\frac{f(X_i)}{p(X_i)} = \frac{1}{c} = \int f(x)\,dx
$$

The variance of the estimator is zero! Of course, this is ludicrous since we would not bother using Monte Carlo if we could integrate directly. However, if a density can be found that is similar in shape to \(f\), variance is reduced.

> 这意味着估计量的方差为 0！当然，这只是一个理论上的荒诞极限：如果我们能直接积分，就根本不需要蒙特卡洛了。不过它说明了一件关键的事——只要找到一个形状和 \(f\) 足够接近的密度函数，方差就能被有效压低。

As a more realistic example, consider the Gaussian function \(f(x) = e^{-1000(x - 1/2)^2}\), which is plotted in Figure [2.2](#fig:gaussian-pdf-and-samples) (a) over \([0,1]\). Its value is close to zero over most of the domain. Samples with \(x < 0.2\) or \(x > 0.8\) are of little help in estimating the value of the integral since they give no information about the magnitude of the bump in the function’s value around \(1/2\). With uniform sampling and the basic Monte Carlo estimator, variance is approximately \(0.0365\).

> 看一个更现实的例子：考虑高斯函数 \(f(x) = e^{-1000(x - 1/2)^2}\)，它在区间 \([0,1]\) 上的图像如图 [2.2](#fig:gaussian-pdf-and-samples) 的（a）所示。这个函数在大部分定义域上都非常接近 0。像 \(x < 0.2\) 或 \(x > 0.8\) 这样的样本，对于估计积分值几乎没有帮助，因为它们无法告诉我们函数在 \(1/2\) 附近那个尖峰的大小。若使用均匀采样与基本蒙特卡洛估计量，其方差大约是 \(0.0365\)。

If samples are instead drawn from the piecewise-constant distribution

> 如果改为从下面这个分段常数分布中采样：

$$
p(x) =
\begin{cases}
0.1, & x \in [0,0.45) \\
9.1, & x \in [0.45,0.55) \\
0.1, & x \in [0.55,1)
\end{cases}
$$

which is plotted in Figure [2.2](#fig:gaussian-pdf-and-samples) (b), and the estimator from Equation ([2.7](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/Monte_Carlo_Basics.html#eq:MC-estimator)) is used instead, then variance is reduced by a factor of approximately 6.7. A representative set of 6 points from this distribution is shown in Figure [2.2](#fig:gaussian-pdf-and-samples) (c); we can see that most of the evaluations of \(f\) are in the interesting region where it is not nearly zero.

> 这个分布在图 [2.2](#fig:gaussian-pdf-and-samples) 的（b）中画了出来。如果使用它并配合式（[2.7](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/Monte_Carlo_Basics.html#eq:MC-estimator)）中的估计量，那么方差大约会下降 6.7 倍。图 [2.2](#fig:gaussian-pdf-and-samples) 的（c）给出了从该分布中抽取的 6 个代表性样本点；可以看到，大多数 \(f\) 的评估都集中在那个“真正有信息量”的非零区域。

[![](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/narrow-gaussian.svg)](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/narrow-gaussian.svg)

[![](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/piecewise-gaussian-pdf.svg)](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/piecewise-gaussian-pdf.svg)

[![](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/piecewise-gaussian-samples.svg)](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/piecewise-gaussian-samples.svg)

Figure 2.2: (a) A narrow Gaussian function that is close to zero over most of the range. The basic Monte Carlo estimator of Equation ([2.6](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/Monte_Carlo_Basics.html#eq:mc-uniform-estimator)) has relatively high variance if it is used to integrate this function, since most samples have values that are close to zero. (b) A PDF that roughly approximates the function’s distribution. If this PDF is used to generate samples, variance is reduced substantially. (c) A representative distribution of samples generated according to (b).

> 图 2.2：（a）一个在大部分区间内都接近 0 的窄高斯函数。如果用式（[2.6](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/Monte_Carlo_Basics.html#eq:mc-uniform-estimator)）中的基本蒙特卡洛估计量去积分它，方差会相对较高，因为大多数样本对应的函数值都接近 0。（b）一个大致贴近该函数分布的 PDF；若用它来生成样本，方差会显著降低。（c）按照（b）所生成的一组代表性样本分布。

Importance sampling can increase variance if a poorly chosen distribution is used, however. Consider instead using the distribution

> 不过，如果采样分布选得不好，重要性采样反而可能增大方差。比如改用下面这个分布：

$$
p(x) =
\begin{cases}
1.2, & x \in [0,0.4) \\
0.2, & x \in [0.4,0.6) \\
1.2, & x \in [0.6,1)
\end{cases}
$$

for estimating the integral of the Gaussian function. This PDF increases the probability of sampling the function where its value is close to zero and decreases the probability of sampling it where its magnitude is larger.

> 来估计这个高斯函数的积分。这个 PDF 会提高在函数值接近 0 的区域采样的概率，却降低在函数值较大的区域采样的概率。

Not only does this PDF generate fewer samples where the integrand is large, but when it does, the magnitude of \(f(x)/p(x)\) in the Monte Carlo estimator will be especially high since \(p(x) = 0.2\) in that region. The result is approximately 5.4 times higher variance than uniform sampling, and nearly 36 times higher variance than the better PDF above. In the context of Monte Carlo integration for rendering where evaluating the integrand generally involves the expense of tracing a ray, it is desirable to minimize the number of samples taken; using an inferior sampling distribution and making up for it by evaluating more samples is an unappealing option.

> 这个 PDF 不仅让“大值区域”的样本更少，而且一旦真的在这些区域采到样本，由于那里 \(p(x)=0.2\)，蒙特卡洛估计量中的 \(f(x)/p(x)\) 会变得特别大。结果是，它的方差大约比均匀采样高 5.4 倍，比上面那个更好的 PDF 高将近 36 倍。在渲染里的蒙特卡洛积分中，评估被积函数通常意味着要付出追踪光线的代价，因此我们通常希望样本数越少越好；使用一个糟糕的采样分布，再靠多取样来补救，是很不划算的。

### 2.2.3 Multiple Importance Sampling

> ### 2.2.3 多重重要性采样

We are frequently faced with integrals that are the product of two or more functions: \(\int f_a(x) f_b(x)\,dx\). It is often possible to derive separate importance sampling strategies for individual factors individually, though not one that is similar to their product. This situation is especially common in the integrals involved with light transport, such as in the product of BSDF, incident radiance, and a cosine factor in the light transport equation ([1.1](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Introduction/Photorealistic_Rendering_and_the_Ray-Tracing_Algorithm.html#eq:rendering-equation)).

> 我们经常会遇到这样的积分：它是两个或多个函数的乘积，例如 \(\int f_a(x) f_b(x)\,dx\)。通常我们可以分别为单个因子设计对应的重要性采样策略，但却很难直接找到一个同时贴近它们乘积形状的采样分布。这在光传输相关积分中尤其常见，比如光传输方程（[1.1](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Introduction/Photorealistic_Rendering_and_the_Ray-Tracing_Algorithm.html#eq:rendering-equation)）里 BSDF、入射辐射度和余弦因子的乘积。

To understand the challenges involved with applying Monte Carlo to such products, assume for now the good fortune of having two sampling distributions \(p_a\) and \(p_b\) that match the distributions of \(f_a\) and \(f_b\) exactly. (In practice, this will not normally be the case.) With the Monte Carlo estimator of Equation ([2.7](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/Monte_Carlo_Basics.html#eq:MC-estimator)), we have two options: we might draw samples using \(p_a\), which gives the estimator

> 为了理解把蒙特卡洛方法用于这种乘积时会遇到什么困难，先假设我们非常幸运，恰好拥有两个采样分布 \(p_a\) 和 \(p_b\)，它们分别与 \(f_a\) 和 \(f_b\) 的分布完全匹配。（现实中通常做不到这一点。）在使用式（[2.7](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/Monte_Carlo_Basics.html#eq:MC-estimator)）中的蒙特卡洛估计量时，我们有两个看似自然的选择：例如从 \(p_a\) 采样，就会得到估计量

$$
\frac{f(X)}{p_a(X)} = \frac{f_a(X) f_b(X)}{p_a(X)} = c\,f_b(X)
$$

where \(c\) is a constant equal to the integral of \(f_a\), since \(p_a(x) \propto f_a(x)\). The variance of this estimator is proportional to the variance of \(f_b\), which may itself be high. Conversely, we might sample from \(p_b\), though doing so gives us an estimator with variance proportional to the variance of \(f_a\), which may similarly be high. In the more common case where the sampling distributions only approximately match one of the factors, the situation is usually even worse.

> 其中 \(c\) 是一个常数，等于 \(f_a\) 的积分值，因为 \(p_a(x) \propto f_a(x)\)。这个估计量的方差与 \(f_b\) 的方差成正比，而后者本身可能就很大。反过来，如果从 \(p_b\) 采样，那么得到的估计量方差又会与 \(f_a\) 的方差成正比，也可能同样很大。更常见的是，采样分布只能“大致贴近”某个因子，此时情况通常只会更糟。

Unfortunately, the obvious solution of taking some samples from each distribution and averaging the two estimators is not much better. Because variance is additive, once variance has crept into an estimator, we cannot eliminate it by adding it to another low-variance estimator.

> 不幸的是，一个看起来很自然的办法——从每个分布都取一些样本，然后把两个估计量平均——其实也没好到哪里去。因为方差是可加的，一旦某个估计量里已经混入了较大的方差，仅仅把它和另一个低方差估计量相加，并不能把它“抵消掉”。

Multiple importance sampling (MIS) addresses exactly this issue, with an easy-to-implement variance reduction technique. The basic idea is that, when estimating an integral, we should draw samples from multiple sampling distributions, chosen in the hope that at least one of them will match the shape of the integrand reasonably well, even if we do not know which one this will be. MIS then provides a method to weight the samples from each technique that can eliminate large variance spikes due to mismatches between the integrand’s value and the sampling density. Specialized sampling routines that only account for unusual special cases are even encouraged, as they reduce variance when those cases occur, with relatively little cost in general.

> 多重重要性采样（MIS）正是为了解决这个问题而提出的一种易于实现的降方差技术。它的核心思想是：在估计积分时，我们不应只依赖一种采样分布，而是从多种分布中都取样，希望其中至少有一种会与被积函数的形状较为匹配，即便我们事先并不知道是哪一种。MIS 接着通过一套加权方法来组合各个技术产生的样本，从而消除因“被积函数值与采样密度不匹配”而产生的大方差尖峰。甚至那些只针对某些特殊场景设计的专门采样方法也值得鼓励，因为在那些场景出现时，它们往往能以不高的额外代价显著降低方差。

With two sampling distributions \(p_a\) and \(p_b\) and a single sample taken from each one, \(X \sim p_a\) and \(Y \sim p_b\), the MIS Monte Carlo estimator is

> 假设有两个采样分布 \(p_a\) 与 \(p_b\)，并且从每个分布中各取一个样本，即 \(X \sim p_a\)、\(Y \sim p_b\)，那么 MIS 的蒙特卡洛估计量为：

([2.13](#eq:mis-single-simple-two))

$$
w_a(X)\frac{f(X)}{p_a(X)} + w_b(Y)\frac{f(Y)}{p_b(Y)}
$$

where \(w_a\) and \(w_b\) are weighting functions chosen such that the expected value of this estimator is the value of the integral of \(f\).

> 其中 \(w_a\) 与 \(w_b\) 是加权函数，它们被选取得使这个估计量的期望值恰好等于 \(f\) 的积分值。

More generally, given \(n\) sampling distributions with \(n_i\) samples taken from the \(i\)th distribution, the MIS Monte Carlo estimator is

> 更一般地说，如果我们有 \(n\) 个采样分布，并且从第 \(i\) 个分布中取了 \(n_i\) 个样本，那么 MIS 蒙特卡洛估计量为：

$$
F_n = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{n_i} \sum_{j=1}^{n_i} w_i(X_{i,j}) \frac{f(X_{i,j})}{p_i(X_{i,j})}
$$

(The full set of conditions on the weighting functions for the estimator to be unbiased are that they sum to 1 when \(f(x) \neq 0\), and that if \(p_i(x)=0\), then \(w_i(x)=0\).)

> （为了让估计量保持无偏，加权函数需要满足完整条件：当 \(f(x) \neq 0\) 时，各个 \(w_i(x)\) 的和必须为 1；并且若 \(p_i(x)=0\)，则必须有 \(w_i(x)=0\)。）

Setting \(w_i(X) = 1/n\) corresponds to the case of summing the various estimators, which we have already seen is an ineffective way to reduce variance. It would be better if the weighting functions were relatively large when the corresponding sampling technique was a good match to the integrand and relatively small when it was not, thus reducing the contribution of high-variance samples.

> 如果简单地令 \(w_i(X)=1/n\)，那就退化成“把多个估计量直接相加平均”的情形，而我们刚刚已经看到，这并不是降低方差的有效办法。更理想的情况是：当某种采样技术与被积函数匹配得好时，它对应的权重应当较大；匹配不好时，权重则应较小。这样就能削弱那些高方差样本的贡献。

In practice, a good choice for the weighting functions is given by the *balance heuristic*, which attempts to fulfill this goal by taking into account all the different ways that a sample could have been generated, rather than just the particular one that was used to do so. The balance heuristic’s weighting function for the \(i\)th sampling technique is

> 在实践中，一个很好的权重选择是*平衡启发式*（balance heuristic）。它试图通过“考虑一个样本可能由哪些不同方式生成”，而不只是考虑“它实际上是由哪一种方式生成”，来实现上述目标。对于第 \(i\) 个采样技术，平衡启发式给出的权重函数是：

([2.14](#eq:balance-heuristic))

$$
w_i(x) = \frac{n_i p_i(x)}{\sum_j n_j p_j(x)}
$$

With the balance heuristic and our example of taking a single sample from each of two sampling techniques, the estimator of Equation ([2.13](#eq:mis-single-simple-two)) works out to be

> 结合平衡启发式，并回到“两个采样技术各取一个样本”的例子，式（[2.13](#eq:mis-single-simple-two)）中的估计量可以化简为：

$$
\frac{f(X)}{p_a(X) + p_b(X)} + \frac{f(Y)}{p_a(Y) + p_b(Y)}
$$

Each evaluation of \(f\) is divided by the sum of all PDFs for the corresponding sample rather than just the one that generated the sample. Thus, if \(p_a\) generates a sample with low probability at a point where \(p_b\) has a higher probability, then dividing by \(p_a(X) + p_b(X)\) reduces the sample’s contribution. Effectively, such samples are downweighted when sampled from \(p_a\), recognizing that the sampling technique associated with \(p_b\) is more effective at the corresponding point in the integration domain. As long as just one of the sampling techniques has a reasonable probability of sampling a point where the function’s value is large, the MIS weights can lead to a significant reduction in variance.

> 这里每次对 \(f\) 的求值，都不是只除以“生成该样本的那一个 PDF”，而是除以“该点处所有相关 PDF 的和”。因此，如果 \(p_a\) 在某个点以较低概率生成了样本，而 \(p_b\) 在该点概率更高，那么分母中的 \(p_a(X)+p_b(X)\) 就会压低这个样本的贡献。换句话说，这类“明明不该由 \(p_a\) 来采、却偏偏由它采到了”的样本，会被自动降权，因为系统认识到 \(p_b\) 在那个位置上更适合采样。只要多种采样技术中有至少一种在函数值较大的区域拥有合理采样概率，MIS 权重就往往能显著降低方差。

BalanceHeuristic() computes Equation ([2.14](#eq:balance-heuristic)) for the specific case of two distributions \(f\) and \(g\). We will not need a more general multidistribution case in pbrt.

> `BalanceHeuristic()` 实现了式（[2.14](#eq:balance-heuristic)）在“两种分布 \(f\) 与 \(g\)”这个特例下的计算。pbrt 并不需要一个更一般的多分布版本。

```cpp
<<Sampling Inline Functions>>=
Float BalanceHeuristic(int nf, Float fPdf, int ng, Float gPdf) {
    return (nf * fPdf) / (nf * fPdf + ng * gPdf);
}
```

In practice, the *power heuristic* often reduces variance even further. For an exponent \(\beta\), the power heuristic is

> 在实践中，*幂启发式*（power heuristic）往往还能进一步降低方差。对于指数 \(\beta\)，它的形式为：

([2.15](#eq:mis-power-heuristic))

$$
w_i(x) = \frac{(n_i p_i(x))^\beta}{\sum_j (n_j p_j(x))^\beta}
$$

Note that the power heuristic has a similar form to the balance heuristic, though it further reduces the contribution of relatively low probabilities. Our implementation has \(\beta = 2\) hard-coded in its implementation; that parameter value usually works well in practice.

> 可以看到，幂启发式与平衡启发式形式相近，但它会进一步压低那些相对较小概率项的贡献。在 pbrt 的实现里，\(\beta = 2\) 是直接写死的；这个参数值在实践中通常效果很好。

```cpp
Float PowerHeuristic(int nf, Float fPdf, int ng, Float gPdf) {
    Float f = nf * fPdf, g = ng * gPdf;
    return Sqr(f) / (Sqr(f) + Sqr(g));
}
```

Multiple importance sampling can be applied even without sampling from all the distributions. This approach is known as the *single sample model*. We will not include the derivation here, but it can be shown that given an integrand \(f(x)\), if a sampling technique is chosen from a set of techniques with probability \(q_i\) and a sample is drawn from \(p_i\), then the *single sample estimator*

> 多重重要性采样甚至可以在“不必从所有分布都采样”的情况下使用，这种做法称为*单样本模型*（single sample model）。这里不展开推导，但可以证明：对给定的被积函数 \(f(x)\)，如果我们以概率 \(q_i\) 从若干采样技术中选中第 \(i\) 个技术，再从 \(p_i\) 中抽出样本，那么下面这个*单样本估计量*

([2.16](#eq:mis-single-sample-estimator))

$$
\frac{w_i(X)}{q_i} \frac{f(X)}{p_i(X)}
$$

gives an unbiased estimate of the integral. For the single sample model, the balance heuristic is provably optimal.

> 依然会给出该积分的无偏估计。对于单样本模型，平衡启发式可以被证明是最优的。

One shortcoming of multiple importance sampling is that if one of the sampling techniques is a very good match to the integrand, MIS can slightly increase variance. For rendering applications, MIS is almost always worthwhile for the variance reduction it provides in cases that can otherwise have high variance.

> 多重重要性采样的一个缺点是：如果某一种采样技术本来就已经与被积函数极其匹配，那么 MIS 反而可能稍微增加一点方差。不过在渲染应用里，它几乎总是值得的，因为它在那些原本可能出现高方差的情况中带来的收益通常远远超过这个代价。

#### MIS Compensation

> #### MIS 补偿

Multiple importance sampling is generally applied using probability distributions that are all individually valid for importance sampling the integrand, with nonzero probability of generating a sample anywhere that the integrand is nonzero. However, when MIS is being used, it is not a requirement that all PDFs are nonzero where the function’s value is nonzero; only one of them must be.

> 一般来说，MIS 会配合若干“各自单独看也能对被积函数做重要性采样”的概率分布来使用，也就是说，只要被积函数非零，这些 PDF 通常都会在对应位置给出非零采样概率。然而在 MIS 的语境下，其实并不要求所有 PDF 在函数非零的地方都非零；只要其中至少有一个满足这一点就够了。

This observation led to the development of a technique called *MIS compensation*, which can further reduce variance. It is motivated by the fact that if all the sampling distributions allocate some probability to sampling regions where the integrand’s value is small, it is often the case that that region of the integrand ends up being oversampled, leaving the region where the integrand is high undersampled.

> 这一观察催生了一种叫做 *MIS compensation*（MIS 补偿）的技术，它还能进一步降低方差。它背后的动机是：如果所有采样分布都会把一部分概率浪费在“被积函数值很小”的区域上，那么这些区域往往会被过度采样，而真正函数值高、最需要样本支持的区域却反而采样不足。

MIS compensation is based on the idea of sharpening one or more (but not all) the probability distributions—for example, by adjusting them to have zero probability in areas where they earlier had low probability. A new sampling distribution can, for example, be defined by

> MIS 补偿的思路是：把一个或多个（但不是全部）概率分布“锐化”。例如，可以把它们在原本只有很小概率的区域直接削成 0。举例来说，可以定义一个新的采样分布：

$$
p'(x) = \frac{\max(0, p(x) - \delta)}{\int \max(0, p(x) - \delta)\,dx}
$$

for some fixed value \(\delta\).

> 其中 \(\delta\) 是某个固定阈值。

This technique is especially easy to apply in the case of tabularized sampling distributions. In Section [12.5](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Light_Sources/Infinite_Area_Lights.html#sec:infinite-area-lights), it is used to good effect for sampling environment map light sources.

> 这种技术在“表格化”的采样分布上尤其容易使用。在第 [12.5](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Light_Sources/Infinite_Area_Lights.html#sec:infinite-area-lights) 节中，它被非常有效地用于环境贴图光源的采样。

### 2.2.4 Russian Roulette

> ### 2.2.4 俄罗斯轮盘赌

Russian roulette is a technique that can improve the efficiency of Monte Carlo estimates by skipping the evaluation of samples that would make a small contribution to the final result. In rendering, we often have estimators of the form

> 俄罗斯轮盘赌是一种通过“跳过那些对最终结果贡献很小的样本评估”来提高蒙特卡洛效率的技术。在渲染中，我们经常会遇到如下形式的估计量：

$$
\frac{f(X) v(X)}{p(X)}
$$

where the integrand consists of some factors that are easily evaluated (e.g., those that relate to how the surface scatters light) and others that are more expensive to evaluate, such as a binary visibility factor that requires tracing a ray. In these cases, most of the computational expense of evaluating the estimator lies in \(v\).

> 其中被积函数由两部分组成：一部分容易计算（例如与表面如何散射光有关的因子），另一部分则代价昂贵，比如一个需要通过追踪光线来判断的二值可见性因子。在这种情况下，评估整个估计量的大部分计算开销都集中在 \(v\) 上。

If \(f\) is zero, it is obviously worth skipping the work of evaluating \(v\), since its value will not affect the value of the estimator. However, if we also skipped evaluating estimators where \(f\) was small but nonzero, then we would introduce bias into the estimator and would systemically underestimate the value of the integrand. Russian roulette solves this problem, making it possible to also skip tracing rays when \(f\)'s value is small but not necessarily 0, while still computing the correct value on average.

> 如果 \(f\) 恰好为 0，那么显然值得直接跳过 \(v\) 的计算，因为它不会影响估计量的结果。然而，如果我们连那些 \(f\) 很小但不为 0 的情况也一并跳过，就会给估计量引入偏差，并系统性地低估被积函数的值。俄罗斯轮盘赌解决的正是这个问题：即使 \(f\) 很小但不一定为 0，我们也可以有选择地跳过追踪光线，同时仍然保证从平均意义上得到正确结果。

To apply Russian roulette, we select some termination probability \(q\). This value can be chosen in almost any manner; for example, it could be based on an estimate of the value of the integrand for the particular sample chosen, increasing as the integrand’s value becomes smaller. With probability \(q\), the estimator is not evaluated for the particular sample, and some constant value is used in its place (\(c = 0\) is often used). With probability \(1-q\), the estimator is still evaluated but is weighted by the factor \(1/(1-q)\), which effectively compensates for the samples that were skipped.

> 使用俄罗斯轮盘赌时，我们先选定一个终止概率 \(q\)。它几乎可以按任意方式设定；例如，可以根据当前样本对应的被积函数估值来决定，让该值越小，终止概率越高。以概率 \(q\)，这个样本的估计量不再被真正计算，而是直接用某个常数值代替（通常取 \(c=0\)）；以概率 \(1-q\)，则仍然继续计算它，但要额外乘上因子 \(1/(1-q)\)，从而对那些被跳过的样本进行补偿。

We have the new estimator

> 于是我们得到新的估计量：

$$
F' =
\begin{cases}
\dfrac{F - qc}{1-q}, & \xi > q \\
c, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

It is easy to see that its expected value is the same as the expected value of the original estimator:

> 很容易验证，它的期望值与原始估计量完全相同：

$$
E[F'] = (1-q)\left(\frac{E[F]-qc}{1-q}\right) + qc = E[F]
$$

Russian roulette never reduces variance. In fact, unless somehow \(c = F\), it will always increase variance. However, it does improve Monte Carlo efficiency if the probabilities are chosen so that samples that are likely to make a small contribution to the final result are skipped.

> 俄罗斯轮盘赌本身并不会降低方差。事实上，除非出现 \(c=F\) 这种几乎不现实的特殊情形，否则它总会提高方差。不过，只要我们把概率选得合理，使那些原本只会贡献极小值的样本被跳过，那么它仍然能提升整体的蒙特卡洛效率。

### 2.2.5 Splitting

> ### 2.2.5 分裂

While Russian roulette reduces the number of samples, splitting increases the number of samples in some dimensions of multidimensional integrals in order to improve efficiency. As an example, consider an integral of the general form

> 如果说俄罗斯轮盘赌是在减少样本数，那么分裂（splitting）则是在某些多维积分维度上增加样本数，以此提升效率。举个例子，考虑下面这种一般形式的积分：

([2.17](#eq:splitting-candidate-integral))

$$
\int_A \int_B f(x,y)\,dx\,dy
$$

With the standard importance sampling estimator, we might draw samples from independent distributions, \(X_i \sim p_x\) and \(Y_i \sim p_y\), and compute

> 用标准的重要性采样估计量时，我们可能会从两个独立分布中分别取样，即 \(X_i \sim p_x\) 和 \(Y_i \sim p_y\)，然后计算：

([2.18](#eq:splitting-std-estimator))

$$
\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{f(X_i,Y_i)}{p_x(X_i)p_y(Y_i)}
$$

Splitting allows us to formalize the idea of taking more than one sample for the integral over \(B\) for each sample taken in \(A\). With splitting, we might take \(m\) samples \(Y_{i,j}\) for each sample \(X_i\), giving the estimator

> 分裂把这样一个直觉形式化了：对 \(A\) 上的每一个样本，不只在 \(B\) 上取一个样本，而是取多个样本。也就是说，对于每个 \(X_i\)，我们再额外取 \(m\) 个 \(Y_{i,j}\)，从而得到估计量：

$$
\frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{1}{m} \sum_{j=1}^{m} \frac{f(X_i,Y_{i,j})}{p_x(X_i)p_y(Y_{i,j})}
$$

If it is possible to partially evaluate \(f\) for each \(X_i\), then we can compute a total of \(nm\) samples more efficiently than we had taken independent values using Equation ([2.18](#eq:splitting-std-estimator)).

> 如果对于每个 \(X_i\)，我们都能先把 \(f\) 的一部分计算结果复用起来，那么总共计算 \(nm\) 个样本，会比直接按式（[2.18](#eq:splitting-std-estimator)）那样独立取 \(nm\) 组完整样本更高效。

For an example from rendering, an integral of the form of Equation ([2.17](#eq:splitting-candidate-integral)) is evaluated to compute the color of pixels in an image: an integral is taken over the area of the pixel where at each point in the pixel, a ray is traced into the scene and the reflected radiance at the intersection point is computed using an integral over the hemisphere (denoted here by \(B\)) for which one or more rays are traced. With splitting, we can take multiple samples for each lighting integral, improving efficiency by amortizing the cost of tracing the initial ray from the camera over them.

> 在渲染里，一个典型例子就是按式（[2.17](#eq:splitting-candidate-integral)）这种形式的积分来计算像素颜色：我们先在像素面积上积分；对于像素中的每个点，都会向场景中追踪一条光线；到达交点后，又需要通过对半球（这里记作 \(B\)）上的积分来估计反射辐射度，而这一步还会继续追踪一条或多条光线。使用分裂后，我们可以针对每次光照积分取多个样本，从而把“从相机发出第一条光线”的开销摊薄到多个后续样本上，提高总体效率。
