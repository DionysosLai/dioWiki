## 2.1 Monte Carlo: Basics

> ## 2.1 蒙特卡洛：基础

Because Monte Carlo integration is based on randomization, we will start this chapter with a brief review of ideas from probability and statistics that provide the foundations of the approach. Doing so will allow us to introduce the basic Monte Carlo algorithm as well as mathematical tools for evaluating its error.

> 由于蒙特卡洛积分建立在随机化之上，我们将以对概率论与统计学中若干基础思想的简要回顾开始本章。这些内容将帮助我们引入最基本的蒙特卡洛算法，以及用于评估其误差的数学工具。

### 2.1.1 Background and Probability Review

> ### 2.1.1 背景与概率回顾

We will start by defining some terms and reviewing basic ideas from probability. We assume that the reader is already familiar with basic probability concepts; readers needing a more complete introduction to this topic should consult a textbook such as Sheldon Ross’s *Introduction to Probability Models* ([2002](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/Further_Reading.html#cite:Ross:ProbabilityModels)).

> 我们先定义一些术语，并回顾概率论中的基本思想。这里默认读者已经熟悉基础概率概念；如果你需要更完整的入门介绍，可以参考 Sheldon Ross 的 *Introduction to Probability Models*（[2002](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/Further_Reading.html#cite:Ross:ProbabilityModels)）这类教材。

A *random variable* is a value chosen by some random process. We will generally use capital letters to denote random variables, with exceptions made for a few Greek symbols that represent special random variables. Random variables are always drawn from some domain, which can be either discrete (e.g., a fixed, finite set of possibilities) or continuous (e.g., the real numbers). Applying a function to a random variable results in a new random variable.

> *随机变量*是由某个随机过程选出的一个值。我们通常用大写字母表示随机变量，不过也会有少数希腊字母用来表示特殊的随机变量。随机变量总是从某个定义域中取值，这个定义域可以是离散的（例如一个固定且有限的可能值集合），也可以是连续的（例如实数域）。对一个随机变量施加函数，会得到一个新的随机变量。

For example, the result of a roll of a die is a discrete random variable sampled from the set of events . Each event has a probability, and the sum of probabilities is necessarily one. A random variable like this one that has the same probability for all potential values of it is said to be *uniform*. A function that gives a discrete random variable’s probability is termed a *probability mass function* (PMF), and so we could equivalently write  in this case.

> 例如，掷一次骰子的结果就是一个离散随机变量，它从事件集合 
> \(\{1,2,3,4,5,6\}\) 中取值。每个事件都有对应的概率，而且所有概率之和必然为 1。像这样所有可能取值都具有相同概率的随机变量，被称为*均匀*随机变量。给出离散随机变量各取值概率的函数称为*概率质量函数*（PMF），因此在这个例子里也可以把它写成对每个结果都相同的概率函数。

$$
p_i = \frac{1}{6}, \qquad \sum_i p_i = 1
$$

$$
p(X) = \frac{1}{6}
$$

Two random variables are *independent* if the probability of one does not affect the probability of the other. In this case, the *joint probability* of two random variables is given by the product of their probabilities:

> 如果两个随机变量中一个的概率不会影响另一个的概率，那么它们就是*独立*的。在这种情况下，两个随机变量的*联合概率*等于它们各自概率的乘积：

$$
p(X,Y) = p(X)\,p(Y)
$$

For example, two random variables representing random samples of the six sides of a die are independent.

> 例如，用两个随机变量分别表示两次掷骰子的结果时，这两个随机变量就是独立的。

For *dependent* random variables, one’s probability affects the other’s. Consider a bag filled with some number of black balls and some number of white balls. If we randomly choose two balls from the bag, the probability of the second ball being white is affected by the color of the first ball since its choice changes the number of balls of one type left in the bag. We will say that the second ball’s probability is *conditioned* on the choice of the first one. In this case, the joint probability for choosing two balls  and  is given by

> 对于*相依*随机变量，一个变量的概率会影响另一个变量的概率。想象一个袋子里装着若干黑球和白球。如果我们从中随机取出两个球，那么第二个球是白球的概率会受到第一个球颜色的影响，因为第一次取球会改变袋中剩余球的数量。我们会说，第二个球的概率是以第一个球的选择为*条件*的。在这种情况下，选择两个球 \(X\) 与 \(Y\) 的联合概率可写为：

([2.1](#eq:conditional-2d-density))

$$
p(X,Y) = p(X)\,p(Y\mid X)
$$

where  is the *conditional probability* of  given a value of .

> 其中 \(p(Y\mid X)\) 表示：在 \(X\) 取某个给定值的条件下，\(Y\) 的*条件概率*。

In the following, it will often be the case that a random variable’s probability is conditioned on many values; for example, when choosing a light source from which to sample illumination, the [BVHLightSampler](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Light_Sources/Light_Sampling.html#BVHLightSampler) in Section [12.6.3](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Light_Sources/Light_Sampling.html#sec:bvh-light-sampling) considers the 3D position of the receiving point and its surface normal, and so the choice of light is conditioned on them. However, we will often omit the variables that a random variable is conditioned on in cases where there are many of them and where enumerating them would obscure notation.

> 在后文中，随机变量的概率往往会同时依赖许多条件值。例如，在为照明采样而选择光源时，第 [12.6.3](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Light_Sources/Light_Sampling.html#sec:bvh-light-sampling) 节中的 [BVHLightSampler](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Light_Sources/Light_Sampling.html#BVHLightSampler) 会考虑接收点的三维位置以及其表面法线，因此光源的选择是以这些量为条件的。不过在很多场景下，如果条件变量太多，把它们全部写出来反而会让记号变得混乱，所以后文常常会省略这些条件变量。

For lighting applications, we might want to define the probability of sampling illumination from each light in the scene based on its power relative to the total power from all sources:

> 在光照应用中，我们可能希望根据每个光源的功率占场景总光功率的比例，来定义从各个光源采样照明的概率：

The *cumulative distribution function* (CDF) of a random variable is the probability that a value from the variable’s distribution is less than or equal to some value:

> 随机变量的*累积分布函数*（CDF）表示：从该随机变量分布中抽取的值，小于等于某个给定值的概率：

([2.3](#eq:cdf-definition))

$$
P(x) = \Pr(X \le x)
$$

> 在骰子例子里，![[Pasted image 20260430114151.png]]，因为六种可能结果中有两种小于等于 2。

*Continuous random variables* take on values over ranges of continuous domains (e.g., the real numbers, directions on the unit sphere, or the surfaces of shapes in the scene). Beyond , another example of a continuous random variable is the random variable that ranges over the real numbers between 0 and 2, where the probability of its taking on any particular value is proportional to the value: it is twice as likely for this random variable to take on a value around 0 as it is to take one around 1, and so forth.

> *连续随机变量*会在连续定义域上取值（例如实数、单位球面上的方向，或者场景中物体表面的点）。除了 \(\xi\) 之外，另一个连续随机变量的例子是：它在 0 到 2 之间取值，并且不同位置的取值概率密度并不相同；也就是说，它在某些区域附近出现的概率会比另一些区域更大。

The *probability density function* (PDF) formalizes this idea: it describes the relative probability of a random variable taking on a particular value and is the continuous analog of the PMF. The PDF is the derivative of the random variable’s CDF,

> *概率密度函数*（PDF）把这种想法形式化了：它描述连续随机变量取某个特定值附近的相对概率，是 PMF 在连续情形下的对应物。PDF 就是随机变量 CDF 的导数：

$$
p(x) = \frac{dP(x)}{dx}
$$

For uniform random variables,  is a constant; this is a direct consequence of uniformity. For  we have

> 对于均匀随机变量，\(p(x)\) 是常数；这是“均匀”这一性质的直接结果。对于 \(\xi\)，有：

$$
p(x) =
\begin{cases}
1, & x \in [0,1) \\
0, & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

PDFs are necessarily nonnegative and always integrate to 1 over their domains. Note that their value at a point is *not* necessarily less than 1, however.

> PDF 必须非负，并且在其定义域上的积分恒等于 1。不过要注意，PDF 在某一点处的数值*不一定*小于 1。

Given an interval in the domain, integrating the PDF gives the probability that a random variable lies inside the interval:

> 给定定义域中的一个区间，对 PDF 在该区间上积分，就得到随机变量落在该区间中的概率：

$$
\Pr(x \in [a,b]) = \int_a^b p(x)\,dx = P(b) - P(a)
$$

This follows directly from the first fundamental theorem of calculus and the definition of the PDF.

> 这可以直接由微积分基本定理以及 PDF 的定义推出。

### 2.1.2 Expected Values

> ### 2.1.2 期望值

The *expected value* of a function is defined as the average value of the function over some distribution of values over its domain. It is defined as

> 函数的*期望值*被定义为：在其定义域上的某个概率分布下，这个函数取值的平均值。其定义为：

([2.4](#eq:expected-value))

$$
E_p[f(x)] = \int_D f(x)\,p(x)\,dx
$$

As an example, consider finding the expected value of the cosine function between  and , where  is uniform. Because the PDF must integrate to 1 over the domain, , so

> 例如，考虑在 \([0,\pi]\) 上求余弦函数的期望值，其中随机变量 \(x\) 服从均匀分布。因为 PDF 在整个定义域上的积分必须等于 1，所以这里有 \(p(x)=1/\pi\)，于是：

$$
E[\cos x] = \int_0^{\pi} \cos x\,\frac{1}{\pi}\,dx = \frac{1}{\pi}(\sin\pi - \sin 0) = 0
$$

which is precisely the expected result. (Consider the graph of  over  to see why this is so.)

> 这正是我们预期的结果。（如果你画出 \(\cos x\) 在 \([0,\pi]\) 上的图像，就能直观看出为什么答案是 0。）

The expected value has a few useful properties that follow from its definition:

> 由定义可以直接推出期望值的一些有用性质：

([2.5](#eq:expected-value-properties))

$$
E[a f(x)] = a\,E[f(x)]
$$

$$
E\left[\sum_{i=1}^{n} f(X_i)\right] = \sum_{i=1}^{n} E[f(X_i)]
$$

We will repeatedly use these properties in derivations in the following sections.

> 在后续各节的推导里，我们会反复用到这些性质。

### 2.1.3 The Monte Carlo Estimator

> ### 2.1.3 蒙特卡洛估计量

We can now define the Monte Carlo estimator, which approximates the value of an arbitrary integral. Suppose that we want to evaluate a 1D integral. Given a supply of independent uniform random variables , the Monte Carlo estimator says that the expected value of the estimator

> 现在我们可以定义蒙特卡洛估计量了。它用于近似任意积分的值。假设我们想计算一个一维积分，并且手头有一组彼此独立的、在区间 \([a,b]\) 上均匀分布的随机变量 \(X_i\)。蒙特卡洛估计量告诉我们，下面这个估计量的期望值：

([2.6](#eq:mc-uniform-estimator))

$$
F_n = \frac{b-a}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} f(X_i)
$$

, is equal to the integral. This fact can be demonstrated with just a few steps. First, note that the PDF corresponding to the random variable  must be equal to , since  must not only be a constant but also integrate to 1 over the domain. Algebraic manipulation using the properties from Equations ([2.4](#eq:expected-value)) and ([2.5](#eq:expected-value-properties)) then shows that

> 它恰好等于目标积分。这个结论只要经过几步就能证明。首先注意，与随机变量 \(X_i\) 对应的 PDF 一定是 \(1/(b-a)\)，因为它既必须是常数，又必须在整个区间上积分为 1。再结合式（[2.4](#eq:expected-value)）与（[2.5](#eq:expected-value-properties)）中的期望性质做代数变换，就能得到：

$$
\begin{aligned}
E[F_n]
&= E\left[\frac{b-a}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} f(X_i)\right] \\
&= \frac{b-a}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} E[f(X_i)] \\
&= \frac{b-a}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} \int_a^b f(x)\,p(x)\,dx \\
&= \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} \int_a^b f(x)\,dx \\
&= \int_a^b f(x)\,dx.
\end{aligned}
$$

Extending this estimator to multiple dimensions or complex integration domains is straightforward: independent samples are taken from a uniform multidimensional PDF, and the estimator is applied in the same way. For example, consider the 3D integral

> 把这个估计量扩展到多维积分或更复杂的积分域也很直接：只要从一个均匀的多维 PDF 中抽取独立样本，然后用同样的方法构造估计量即可。例如，考虑下面这个三维积分：

$$
\int_{z_0}^{z_1}\int_{y_0}^{y_1}\int_{x_0}^{x_1} f(x,y,z)\,dx\,dy\,dz
$$

If samples are chosen uniformly from the cube from , then the PDF is the constant value

> 如果样本 \(X_i=(x_i,y_i,z_i)\) 是从长方体区域 
> \([x_0,x_1]\times[y_0,y_1]\times[z_0,z_1]\) 中均匀抽取的，那么对应的 PDF 就是常数：

$$
p(X) = \frac{1}{(x_1-x_0)(y_1-y_0)(z_1-z_0)}
$$

and the estimator is

> 此时估计量为：

$$
\frac{(x_1-x_0)(y_1-y_0)(z_1-z_0)}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} f(X_i)
$$

The restriction to uniform random variables can be relaxed with a small generalization. This is an important step, since carefully choosing the PDF from which samples are drawn leads to a key technique for reducing error in Monte Carlo that will be introduced in Section [2.2.2](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/Improving_Efficiency.html#sec:importance-sampling). If the random variables are drawn from a PDF , then the estimator

> 对“必须使用均匀随机变量”这一限制，只需要做一个很小的推广就可以放宽。这一步非常重要，因为精心选择样本所来自的 PDF，会导向一种在蒙特卡洛中减少误差的关键技术——第 [2.2.2](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/Improving_Efficiency.html#sec:importance-sampling) 节将介绍的*重要性采样*。如果随机变量是从某个 PDF \(p(x)\) 中抽取的，那么可以使用下面的估计量：

([2.7](#eq:MC-estimator))

$$
F_n = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{f(X_i)}{p(X_i)}
$$

can be used to estimate the integral instead. The only limitation on  is that it must be nonzero for all  where .

> 用它同样可以估计目标积分。对 \(p(x)\) 唯一的要求是：凡是 \(|f(x)|>0\) 的地方，\(p(x)\) 都必须非零。

It is similarly not too hard to see that the expected value of this estimator is the desired integral of :

> 同样不难看出，这个估计量的期望值仍然等于我们想要的 \(f\) 的积分：

$$
\begin{aligned}
E[F_n]
&= E\left[\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{f(X_i)}{p(X_i)}\right] \\
&= \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} \int_a^b \frac{f(x)}{p(x)} p(x)\,dx \\
&= \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} \int_a^b f(x)\,dx \\
&= \int_a^b f(x)\,dx.
\end{aligned}
$$

With Monte Carlo, the number of samples can be chosen arbitrarily, regardless of the dimensionality of the integrand. This is another important advantage of Monte Carlo over traditional deterministic quadrature techniques, which typically require a number of samples that is exponential in the dimension.

> 使用蒙特卡洛时，无论被积函数是几维，样本数都可以自由选择。这是它相对于传统确定性求积方法的另一个重要优势，因为后者通常需要随维度指数增长的样本数量。

### 2.1.4 Error in Monte Carlo Estimators

> ### 2.1.4 蒙特卡洛估计量中的误差

Showing that the Monte Carlo estimator converges to the right answer is not enough to justify its use; its rate of convergence is important too. *Variance*, the expected squared deviation of a function from its expected value, is a useful way to characterize Monte Carlo estimators’ convergence. The variance of an estimator is defined as

> 仅仅证明蒙特卡洛估计量会收敛到正确答案，还不足以说明它为什么值得使用；它的收敛速度同样重要。*方差*——也就是一个函数相对其期望值的平方偏差的期望——是刻画蒙特卡洛估计量收敛性质的一种有用方式。一个估计量的方差定义为：

([2.8](#eq:variance-initial))

$$
V[F] = E\big[(F - E[F])^2\big]
$$

from which it follows that

> 由此立刻可得：

$$
V[aF] = a^2 V[F]
$$

This property and Equation ([2.5](#eq:expected-value-properties)) yield an alternative expression for the variance:

> 结合这个性质以及式（[2.5](#eq:expected-value-properties)）中的期望性质，可以得到方差的另一个等价表达式：

([2.9](#eq:variance))

$$
V[F] = E[F^2] - E[F]^2
$$

Thus, the variance is the expected value of the square minus the square of the expected value.

> 也就是说，方差等于“平方的期望”减去“期望的平方”。

If the estimator is a sum of independent random variables (like the Monte Carlo estimator ), then the variance of the sum is the sum of the individual random variables’ variances:

> 如果估计量是若干独立随机变量之和（就像蒙特卡洛估计量 \(F_n\) 那样），那么这个和的方差就等于各个随机变量方差之和：

([2.10](#eq:variance-properties))

$$
V\left[\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i\right] = \sum_{i=1}^{n} V[X_i]
$$

From Equation ([2.10](#eq:variance-properties)) it is easy to show that variance decreases linearly with the number of samples. Because variance is squared error, the error in a Monte Carlo estimate therefore only goes down at a rate of  in the number of samples. Although standard quadrature techniques converge at a faster rate in one dimension, their performance becomes exponentially worse as the dimensionality of the integrand increases, while Monte Carlo’s convergence rate is independent of the dimension, making Monte Carlo the only practical numerical integration algorithm for high-dimensional integrals.

> 从式（[2.10](#eq:variance-properties)）很容易推出：方差会随着样本数线性下降。由于方差对应的是误差的平方，因此蒙特卡洛估计的误差只会以 \(O(n^{-1/2})\) 的速度下降。虽然标准求积方法在一维时收敛更快，但随着被积函数维度提高，它们的表现会指数级恶化；而蒙特卡洛的收敛率与维度无关，因此它几乎是处理高维积分时唯一实际可用的数值积分方法。

The characteristic of Monte Carlo’s rate of error reduction is apparent when watching a progressive rendering of a scene where additional samples are incrementally taken in all pixels. The image improves rapidly for the first few samples when doubling the number of samples is relatively little additional work. Later on, once tens or hundreds of samples have been taken, each additional sample doubling takes much longer and remaining error in the image takes a long time to disappear.

> 在渐进式渲染中，蒙特卡洛这种误差下降速度的特征是非常直观的：当所有像素都不断增加样本时，最开始的几次加样本会让图像改善得很快，因为这时把样本数翻倍所需的额外计算还不算太多；可是一旦样本数已经达到几十或几百，再把样本数翻倍就要花很长时间，而剩余误差也会消失得很慢。

The linear decrease in variance with increasing numbers of samples makes it easy to compare different Monte Carlo estimators. Consider two estimators, where the second has half the variance of the first but takes three times as long to compute an estimate; which of the two is better? In that case, the first is preferable: it could take three times as many samples in the time consumed by the second, in which case it would achieve a  variance reduction. This concept can be encapsulated in the *efficiency* of an estimator, which is defined as

> 由于方差会随着样本数增加而线性下降，因此比较不同的蒙特卡洛估计量就变得比较直接。设想有两个估计量：第二个的方差是第一个的一半，但每次计算却要花三倍时间。哪一个更好？这时反而是第一个更优，因为在第二个花掉的同样时间里，第一个可以多取三倍样本，从而得到更大的总体方差下降。这一想法可以用估计量的*效率*来概括，其定义为：

$$
\epsilon[F] = \frac{1}{V[F] \; T[F]}
$$

where  is its variance and  is the running time to compute its value.

> 其中 \(V[F]\) 是它的方差，\(T[F]\) 是计算其值所需的运行时间。

Not all estimators of integrals have expected values that are equal to the value of the integral. Such estimators are said to be *biased*, where the difference

> 并不是所有积分估计量的期望值都恰好等于积分真值。这样的估计量称为*有偏*估计量，其中差值

$$
\beta = E[F] - \int f(x)\,dx
$$

is the amount of bias. Biased estimators may still be desirable if they are able to get close to the correct result more quickly than unbiased estimators. Kalos and Whitlock ([1986](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/Further_Reading.html#cite:Kalos86), pp. 36–37) gave the following example: consider the problem of computing an estimate of the mean value of a uniform distribution over the interval from 0 to 1. One could use the estimator

> 就是偏差的大小。如果一个有偏估计量能够比无偏估计量更快地逼近正确结果，那么它仍然可能是值得使用的。Kalos 和 Whitlock（[1986](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Monte_Carlo_Integration/Further_Reading.html#cite:Kalos86)，第 36–37 页）给出了下面这个例子：考虑估计区间 \([0,1]\) 上均匀分布均值的问题。我们可以使用无偏估计量：

$$
\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i
$$

or one could use the biased estimator

> 也可以使用下面这个有偏估计量：

$$
\frac{1}{2}\max(X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n)
$$

The first estimator is unbiased but has variance with order . The second estimator’s expected value is

> 第一个估计量是无偏的，但其方差阶为 \(O(n^{-1})\)。第二个估计量的期望值则是：

$$
\frac{1}{2}\frac{n}{n+1} \neq \frac{1}{2}
$$

so it is biased, although its variance is , which is much better. This estimator has the useful property that its error goes to 0 in the limit as the number of samples goes to infinity; such estimators are *consistent*. Most of the Monte Carlo estimators used in pbrt are unbiased, with the notable exception of the SPPMIntegrator, which implements a photon mapping algorithm.

> 因此它是有偏的；不过它的方差阶为 \(O(n^{-2})\)，这要好得多。这个估计量还有一个很有用的性质：当样本数趋于无穷时，它的误差会趋于 0；这样的估计量称为*一致的*（consistent）。在 `pbrt` 中使用的大多数蒙特卡洛估计量都是无偏的，比较显著的例外是实现了光子映射算法的 `SPPMIntegrator`。

Closely related to the variance is the *mean squared error* (MSE), which is defined as the expectation of the squared difference of an estimator and the true value,

> 与方差密切相关的另一个量是*均方误差*（MSE），它定义为估计量与真值之差的平方的期望：

$$
\operatorname{MSE}[F] = E\left[\left(F - \int f(x)\,dx\right)^2\right]
$$

For an unbiased estimator, MSE is equal to the variance; otherwise it is the sum of variance and the squared bias of the estimator.

> 对于无偏估计量，MSE 就等于方差；否则，MSE 等于方差加上偏差平方。

It is possible to work out the variance and MSE of some simple estimators in closed form, but for most of the ones of interest in rendering, this is not possible. Yet it is still useful to be able to quantify these values. For this purpose, the *sample variance* can be computed using a set of independent random variables. Equation ([2.8](#eq:variance-initial)) points at one way to compute the sample variance for a set of random variables. If the *sample mean* is computed as their average, , then the sample variance is

> 对某些简单估计量而言，方差和 MSE 可以写出闭式表达；但对渲染里大多数真正有意义的估计量来说，这通常做不到。即便如此，能够对这些量进行量化依然很有价值。为此，我们可以利用一组独立随机变量来计算*样本方差*。式（[2.8](#eq:variance-initial)）暗示了如何为一组随机变量计算样本方差。如果它们的*样本均值*定义为平均值 \(\bar X\)，那么样本方差就是：

([2.11](#eq:sample-variance-basic))

$$
\bar X = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} X_i
$$

$$
s^2 = \frac{1}{n-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n}(X_i - \bar X)^2
$$

The division by  rather than  is *Bessel’s correction*, and ensures that the sample variance is an unbiased estimate of the variance. (See also Section [B.2.11](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Utilities/Mathematical_Infrastructure.html#sec:robust-variance-estimation), where a numerically stable approach for computing the sample variance is introduced.)

> 这里除以的是 \(n-1\) 而不是 \(n\)，这称为 *Bessel 校正*；它可以保证样本方差是总体方差的无偏估计。（另见第 [B.2.11](https://pbr-book.org/4ed/Utilities/Mathematical_Infrastructure.html#sec:robust-variance-estimation) 节，那里介绍了一个数值上更稳定的样本方差计算方法。）

The sample variance is itself an estimate of the variance, so it has variance itself. Consider, for example, a random variable that has a value of 1 99.99% of the time, and a value of one million 0.01% of the time. If we took ten random samples of it that all had the value 1, the sample variance would suggest that the random variable had zero variance even though its variance is actually much higher.

> 样本方差本身也是对方差的一个估计，因此它自己也会有方差。举个例子：设一个随机变量有 99.99% 的概率取值为 1，而有 0.01% 的概率取值为一百万。如果我们随机抽了 10 个样本，结果恰好全都是 1，那么样本方差会错误地暗示这个随机变量的方差是 0，尽管它真实的方差其实大得多。

If an accurate estimate of the integral can be computed (for example, using a large number of samples), then the mean squared error can be estimated by

> 如果我们能够得到积分的一个足够准确的近似值（例如通过使用大量样本），那么均方误差也可以进一步估计为：

$$
\operatorname{MSE}[F] \approx \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} \big(f(X_i) - \widetilde F\big)^2
$$

The imgtool utility program that is provided in pbrt’s distribution can compute an image’s MSE with respect to a reference image via its diff option.

> `pbrt` 发行版附带的 `imgtool` 工具程序，可以通过 `diff` 选项计算一张图像相对于参考图像的 MSE。
